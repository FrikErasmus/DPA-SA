Bridge Digital Forms

Bridge Digital Forms is a mobile data capture app that can be easily tailored to suit multiple remote data capturing applications. Any type of digital form can be created enabling a user to remotely capture embedded Photos, Video, Text, Signatures, GPS Locations, Barcodes, Maps, Dates and OCR.

Besides forms being easily accessible online and offline, forms can even be pushed to a user, so initiating a task with potential real-time information gathering and reporting.

What Bridge Digital Forms can do

Built for real field conditions — secure access, rich capture, offline reliability, and the operational tools teams need day to day.

Access & workflows

  • Device registration & secure access — API-authenticated onboarding and user/session-aware behavior
  • Task, Survey, Draft & Completed — start, pause, resume, and finalize field work cleanly

Forms & data capture

  • Dynamic forms engine — text, numeric, decimal, date/time, single/multi select, checkboxes, triggers, and sub-questions
  • Rich media capture — camera photos, file attachments, signatures, canvas/sketch, QR scan, and video/watch-required flows
  • Location-aware capture — coordinates, map display, and proximity validation for required geo questions

Offline, sync & notifications

  • Offline-first operation — local database persistence, then background sync when connectivity returns
  • Background sync & retry — pipelines for responses, notifications, and operational logs
  • Push notifications — dev/prod build compatible, with in-app notifications and unread indicators

Operations & field management

  • Operational dashboards — tasks, surveys, drafts, completed, and incident records with category grouping and fast navigation
  • Incident logging — capture field incidents with details and attached media

Reliability & support

  • User-safety — unsaved-changes confirmation before leaving forms, reducing accidental data loss
  • Configurable retention — local data hygiene for predictable storage and cleaner historical data handling
  • Built-in diagnostics — structured logging and sync/error tracking so support teams can troubleshoot quickly

Business value
